**MCM_Master_V06_EN** is the central reference document of the *Monistic Continuum Model (MCM)*.
It defines the unified ontological and geometric framework from which all emergent theories of the
MCM series are derived. The Master Document establishes the foundational axioms, geometric operators,
state definitions, and structural principles of the continuum.

Version V06 refines the geometric ontology, clarifies the role of stress‑induced geometry, and
strengthens the formal separation between ontological assumptions and physically emergent behaviour.
It serves as the authoritative basis for all subsequent MCM publications.

---

## Overview

The Monistic Continuum Model proposes that all observable phenomena arise from the internal dynamics
of a single continuous medium. No fundamental space, time, particles, fields, or forces are assumed.
Instead:

- Geometry is fundamental and induced by stress.
- Physical space emerges only through stable matter defects.
- Rotational and stress‑driven structures generate effective physical behaviour.
- Quantized modes correspond to discrete geometric stability states.
- All macroscopic and microscopic phenomena arise from geometric and rotational structures of the medium.

The Master Document formalizes the medium through a set of dimensionless geometric state variables
representing density, motion, stress, and update behaviour.
