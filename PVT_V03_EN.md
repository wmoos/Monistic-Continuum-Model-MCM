# Overview

**Planck‑Vortex Theory (PVT‑V03)** is a core component of the *Monistic Continuum Model (MCM)*.  
It describes how stable matter arises from rotational–stress configurations within a continuous,
geometry‑inducing medium. PVT interprets the smallest stable geometric structures (“Planck vortices”)
as the foundational defects from which effective mass, charge‑like behaviour, and quantized stability
modes emerge.

---

## Overview

PVT‑V03 provides the geometric and ontological basis for matter formation in the continuum.  
The theory refines:

- the coupling between rotation and stress,
- the stability criteria for vortex‑based defects,
- the geometric operators governing vortex formation,
- and the correspondence between vortex modes and particle‑like behaviour.

These vortices stabilize local geometric domains and act as the seeds of emergent physical space.
