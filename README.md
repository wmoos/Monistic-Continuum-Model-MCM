Version V07 of the Monistic Continuum Model (MCM) represents the further developed and consolidated foundation of a unified ontological and geometric framework in which all observable phenomena arise from the internal dynamics of a continuous medium. This master document defines the central structure, terminology, and theoretical orientation of the entire MCM series and forms the stable core for all subsequent sub‑theories built upon it.

Compared to Version V06, V07 deepens the monistic ontology and refines the geometric interpretation of the internal quantities. The roles of stress, rotation, and curvature as emergent structures of the medium are articulated more clearly, as is the significance of bound geometry for stability, quantization, and matter‑like behavior. The formal presentation of the operators has been unified, the internal coordinates have been specified with greater precision, and the separation between ontological assumptions and derived structures has been sharpened.

Version V07 provides a consistent foundation for analytical development, numerical simulation, and systematic extension of the model.
