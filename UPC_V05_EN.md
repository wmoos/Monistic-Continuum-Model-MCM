## Abstract

The Unified Physical Correspondence (VPK) is the operator-based projection of the Monistic Continuum Model (MCM) onto quantum mechanics (QM) and general relativity (GR). It introduces a discrete vortex basis \(H_w\) and two functorial projections: \(P_{\text{spec}}\) for the spectral (quantum) regime and \(P_{\text{top}}\) for the topological (relativistic) regime. Both arise from a single operator algebra


\[
\mathcal{A} = \text{span}\{G, T, D, S, C\},
\]


ensuring mathematical closure and ontological consistency.

Quantum nonlocality and relativistic locality are shown to be complementary manifestations of the same underlying continuum structure. Axiomatic completeness and proof-oriented consistency are established via the stability lemma


\[
S(W_n) = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \lVert W_n \rVert_{\text{stab}} = \text{const}.
\]


This formulation provides a unified operator framework for transitions between discrete and continuous physical regimes.
