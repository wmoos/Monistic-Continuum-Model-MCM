## **Overview**

**Electron Entanglement (EE‑V04)** is a specialized theoretical module within the *Monistic Continuum Model (MCM)*.  
It describes how electron‑like behaviour emerges from rotational–stress structures of the continuum and how stable
topological defects can exhibit correlated geometric states that correspond to entanglement phenomena.

EE‑V04 provides the geometric interpretation of electron stability, charge‑like behaviour, spin‑like modes, and
non‑local correlations within the unified continuum ontology.

## Overview

EE‑V04 explains electron‑related behaviour without invoking particles, fields, or external quantum postulates.
Instead, it interprets:

- **electron stability** as a stable rotational–stress defect,
- **charge‑like behaviour** as asymmetric rotational geometry,
- **spin‑like modes** as discrete rotational stability states,
- **entanglement** as correlated geometric update behaviour between defects,
- **interaction behaviour** as stress‑mediated coupling through induced geometry.

The theory clarifies how electron‑like structures maintain coherence and how correlated states arise naturally
from the continuum’s internal update dynamics.
