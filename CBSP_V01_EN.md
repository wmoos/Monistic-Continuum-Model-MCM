CBSP‑V01 interprets spectral phenomena as intrinsic geometric processes within the continuum.  
It describes:

- **spectral modes** as stable geometric update patterns,
- **wave‑like behaviour** as rotational‑stress propagation through induced geometry,
- **frequency‑like characteristics** as periodic geometric stability cycles,
- **resonance behaviour** as reinforcement of geometric modes,

- **macroscopic spectral effects** as global stress‑organization patterns.

The theory provides a unified geometric interpretation of spectral behaviour across scales.
