# Overview

**Unified Physical Correspondence (UPC‑V04)** is a core theoretical module within the *Monistic Continuum Model (MCM)*.  
It establishes the correspondence rules that link geometric structures of the continuum to effective
physical behaviour observed at macroscopic and microscopic scales. UPC provides the interpretative
bridge between the internal geometric dynamics of the medium and the familiar constructs of classical,
quantum, and relativistic physics.

Version V04 refines the mapping principles, clarifies the geometric origin of physical observables,
and formalizes the stability‑based interpretation of quantized behaviour.

## Overview

UPC‑V04 defines how physical phenomena emerge from the geometric and stress‑driven structures of the
continuum. It does not introduce external fields, particles, or forces. Instead, it interprets:

- **motion** as geometric update behaviour,
- **mass‑like effects** as stability of topological defects,
- **electromagnetic behaviour** as rotational‑shear modes,
- **quantized states** as discrete geometric stability modes,
- **spatial extension** as stabilized geometric domains,
- **interaction behaviour** as stress‑mediated geometric coupling.

UPC provides the rules that allow these geometric structures to be understood in terms of effective
physical quantities.
